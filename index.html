Rina and the Happy Picnic

Part 1: The sun is shining brightly. Rina wakes up with a big smile. Before she eats breakfast, she closes her eyes and prays to God. "Thank you for this beautiful day," she whispers.

Part 2: Rina walks to school. She sees her friend, Leo. "Oh no, my bag is too heavy!" Leo cries. Rina runs to him. "Don't worry, I will help you," she says. She carries one of his bags.

Part 3: At the park, the children start to argue. "Let's play tag!" says one boy. "No, let's play hide and seek!" says another girl. Rina steps in and says, "Stop, my friends! We are all friends. Let's play together!"

Part 4: To choose the game, all the children sit in a big circle. They talk nicely and listen to each other. They vote for the eagle game. Everyone agrees and they run to the field happily.

Part 5: After playing, everyone is hungry. But, one friend, Sam, looks sad. He forgot his snack. Rina and Leo smile. "Here, Sam! Let's share our food!" Now, everyone has food.

Part 6: Everyone is happy. They gather together and take a group photo under the big tree. What a wonderful day!
